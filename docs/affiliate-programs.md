# Affiliate, partner and referral programs

Research snapshot, checked 2026-09-26. Everything here is "as publicly reported". Many figures come from
the vendor's own partner page as shown in search snippets, and some only from third-party affiliate
directories (marked "3rd-party"). Vendor sites and PartnerStack could not be opened directly from the
research environment, so **confirm every rate, cookie and term inside the program dashboard after
approval** before relying on it. Unknowns are marked `?`.

Program types:

- **Affiliate** = open to publishers, tracked link, pays per signup/sale/lead. Usable by this site.
- **Partner/referral (form)** = you submit a lead or refer a business by form/intro; may pay per demo
  or sale. Sometimes usable by a publisher, often aimed at consultants.
- **Customer referral** = only existing customers can refer, usually for account credit. Not usable by
  this site unless we are a paying customer.

For `partnerNetwork` in `src/content/tools/*.md` the allowed values are `partnerstack`, `impact`,
`cj`, `direct`, `none`.

## Apply first (ranked by likely earnings for this site)

Ranking weighs payout size, whether a publisher can join, and how well the product matches pages the
site already has (reviews, comparisons, alternatives).

1. **Jobber** (PartnerStack) - core product of the site, many existing pages. Reported flat bounties per
   paid plan plus a small per-signup fee; up to 60% of first monthly payment reported by 3rd parties.
2. **Housecall Pro** (PartnerStack) - pays on leads, not only sales: up to $320 per qualified lead,
   $10 per new lead reported, 90-day cookie. Strong fit.
3. **Boulevard** (direct partner program) - up to $1,500 per referral ($750 demo + $750 signup)
   reported. High value per conversion for the salon pages.
4. **ServiceM8** (PartnerStack / direct) - 3rd-party directories report 80% of first 12 months;
   partner agreement shows ongoing revenue share. Verify which applies to publishers.
5. **Workiz** (direct) - "5 customers = $6,000" plus year-end bonus on its partner page. Existing
   comparison pages give it traffic.
6. **Tradify** (direct / PartnerStack) - flat cash bounty per paid customer (reported USD $260).
7. **Gusto** (PartnerStack) - adjacent (payroll), $300 per new payroll customer reported. Good for a
   future "payroll for small trades" page.
8. **Quo (formerly OpenPhone)** (PartnerStack) - 20% for 12 months. Fits the missed-call guide directly.
9. **Thryv** (PartnerStack) - 15% lifetime for affiliates, 90-day cookie reported.
10. **Contractor Foreman** (PartnerStack) - $150 per attended demo + 20% of first purchase.
11. **Momence** (direct partner program) - $250 + $250 per new client in the partner terms.
12. **Mindbody** (direct partner referral) - $1,000 Visa gift card per referred business that signs;
    form-based, so lower volume from a website.

Lower priority but worth an application once traffic exists: NiceJob (recurring), Podium (Impact, per
lead), RingCentral, Square, QuickBooks, Wix, GoDaddy. ServiceTitan is enterprise and referral-based.

## Display ad networks

Entry requirements as currently published or reported (checked 2026-09-26). All networks also review
content quality, originality and policy compliance, and typically want mostly Tier 1 (US, UK, CA, AU,
NZ) traffic.

| Network | Entry requirement (traffic) | Other notes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google AdSense | No published minimum traffic | Must be 18+, own the site and be able to edit its HTML, have enough original content for review, and comply with program policies. | https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724?hl=en |
| Ezoic | 250,000 monthly users for new publishers (from 2026-02-19). Sites using Ezoic before 2026-02-18 are grandfathered. | Ezoic Incubator accepts about 20 publishers a month below the threshold, judged on growth, quality and engagement. Older "Access Now" (no minimum) is described by 3rd-party blogs but appears superseded; confirm. | https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ezoic-raises-bar-to-250k-js-integration-for-full-revenue-platform-surges-in-popularity-with-web-builders-302692672.html ; https://www.ezoic.com/incubator |
| Mediavine Journey | 1,000 sessions per month (from 2026-01-15), sessions from Tier 1 countries within 30 days | No revenue requirement. Reported 70% revenue share, Grow plugin required. Auto-upgrade to full Mediavine at $5,000 ad revenue in trailing 12 months (full program now revenue-based, not the old 50,000 sessions). | https://help.mediavine.com/programs-and-the-publisher-path-to-growth ; https://www.mediavine.com/mediavine-requirements/ |
| Raptive (formerly AdThrive) | 25,000 monthly pageviews (lowered from 100,000 in late 2025) | Sites at 25,000-99,999 pageviews need at least 50% of traffic from US, UK, CA, AU or NZ. Rise pilot retired; entry sites join the Insider tier. | https://help.raptive.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032840891-Who-is-eligible-for-Raptive ; https://www.searchenginejournal.com/raptive-drops-traffic-requirement-by-75-to-25000-views/558780/ |

Practical path for a new site: AdSense (or no display ads) at launch, Mediavine Journey once the site
has 1,000 Tier 1 sessions a month, then Raptive at 25,000 pageviews or full Mediavine once Journey
revenue reaches $5,000 a year. For a B2B review site with low page volume, affiliate revenue will
almost certainly outweigh display ads, and ads on review pages can hurt conversion, so consider
keeping ads off /reviews/ and /compare/ pages.
