## Why do businesses leave Workiz?

Common complaints in public reviews center on contracts, billing and support: annual terms that are hard to exit, charges that continued after cancellation requests, slow replies from support and delays porting phone numbers out. Complaints about the core scheduling and job features are less frequent.

These themes show up across Capterra, Trustpilot and the Better Business Bureau. Workiz also gets positive reviews for dispatching and its phone features, so this is not a verdict on the product itself. It does mean you should read the contract closely whichever tool you choose.

## What is the best Workiz alternative?

For most small home service businesses, [Housecall Pro](/reviews/housecall-pro/) or [Jobber](/reviews/jobber/). Both publish pricing, offer 14-day trials without a card and cover scheduling, dispatch, quoting, invoicing and payments.

Housecall Pro is closer to Workiz on dispatch and also sells a Voice phone system and a CSR AI answering add-on. Jobber is stronger on quoting and has an AI Receptionist add-on for calls and texts. See [Housecall Pro vs Workiz](/compare/housecall-pro-vs-workiz/) and [Jobber vs Workiz](/compare/jobber-vs-workiz/).

## Can I replace the Workiz phone system?

Yes, but plan it. You can use a job tool's own phone or AI answering add-on, or a separate business phone service. The key is to keep your number working throughout the port, since missed calls are expensive for the businesses that choose Workiz in the first place. Our [guide to switching software](/guides/how-to-switch-field-service-software/) covers the order of steps.

## What if I do not need a phone system?

Then you may be paying for features you do not use. [ServiceM8](/reviews/servicem8/) (priced by jobs, unlimited staff, iPhone-first) and [Tradify](/reviews/tradify/) (per user, iOS and Android) are simpler and can cost less for small crews.

Prices change, and Workiz pricing is quote-based, so compare written quotes and confirm on each vendor's site.
