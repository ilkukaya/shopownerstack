## Why do shops leave Workiz?

Usually because they worked out they were not using the phone system. Workiz's premium is
paid for screen pops, recordings and call attribution. A shop whose work arrives by referral
and repeat contract is paying for machinery it never opens.

The other reason is invoicing. Workiz's estimates and invoices are competent and plain, and
a shop that wants to sell harder on paper finds them limiting.

## Before you leave: sort the phone numbers

This is the only migration on this site with a way to genuinely damage the business. Your
main line is hosted at Workiz. Start the port out before you do anything else, keep the
account open until every number has landed at the new carrier, and do not schedule this
during your busy season.

Our port in took eleven days and needed two follow-ups. Assume out is no faster.

## What is the best replacement?

[Housecall Pro](/reviews/housecall-pro/). Comparable dispatch board, much better invoicing
and estimates, on-my-way texts, review requests and consumer financing. You lose call
recording and attribution entirely.

## What if you want to spend less?

[Jobber](/reviews/jobber/) is cheaper at five users and better at quoting, with a calendar
instead of a board. [ServiceM8](/reviews/servicem8/) is cheaper again if you are one to
three vans and can live with a dated interface.

## Should you stay?

If you can name the adverts you buy and Workiz is telling you which ones produce booked
jobs, stay. Nothing else on this site can give you that number, and losing it usually costs
more than the subscription.
