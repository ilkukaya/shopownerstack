## Which is better, Jobber or ServiceM8?

It depends mostly on your phones and your job volume. ServiceM8 is usually the better value for small crews on iPhones with a steady but modest number of jobs, and Jobber is the better choice for Android crews and businesses that live on quotes.

## How is the pricing different?

Jobber charges by plan and number of users. ServiceM8 charges by how many jobs you create each month and includes unlimited staff on every plan.

That difference drives the whole decision. A five-person crew doing a moderate number of larger jobs can pay less on ServiceM8 than on a per-user tool. A one-person business doing many small jobs a day can hit ServiceM8's job caps quickly. According to 2026 pricing guides, ServiceM8 also has a free plan with a small monthly job allowance. Jobber has no free plan but offers a 14-day trial of its Grow plan without a card. Count your actual jobs per month for the last few months before choosing, and confirm current prices on each vendor's site.

## Does ServiceM8 work on Android?

Only partly. ServiceM8's full app runs on iPhone and iPad, and Android users get a lighter version aimed at field staff.

The limited Android app is one of the most frequent complaints in public ServiceM8 reviews, including features such as time tracking that reviewers say are iOS-only. If anyone on your crew uses Android and needs the full app, Jobber is the safer choice.

## Which is better for quoting and customer communication?

Jobber, in our assessment. Its help center documents optional line items on quotes, automated follow-ups for unanswered quotes and a Client Hub where customers approve quotes, pay invoices and request work.

ServiceM8 covers quoting, SMS and email updates, online booking and on-the-way notifications, which is plenty for many trades. Jobber simply goes further on the sales side.

## What do users complain about?

Common complaints in public reviews: for ServiceM8, the Android limitations, chat-only support and a dated interface; for Jobber, reporting depth and features locked behind higher tiers.
