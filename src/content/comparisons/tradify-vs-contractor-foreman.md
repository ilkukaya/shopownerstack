## Which is better, Tradify or Contractor Foreman?

Tradify is better for service and repair trades doing many short jobs, and Contractor Foreman is better for contractors running longer projects. They overlap on quotes, invoices and time tracking but are built for different kinds of work.

## What is each one designed for?

Tradify is job management for trades: quote, schedule, track time, invoice. Contractor Foreman is construction management: project schedules, daily logs, change orders, bids, subcontractors and project financials.

According to its feature pages, Contractor Foreman includes Gantt scheduling with critical path, change orders with online client approval, daily logs, GPS time cards and job costing. Tradify's feature pages focus on scheduling, quoting, invoicing, GPS-stamped timesheets and job costing reports for individual jobs.

## How does pricing compare?

Tradify charges per user per month with unlimited jobs. Contractor Foreman sells tiered plans that each include a number of users, with unlimited projects, billed annually or quarterly.

Third-party 2026 pricing guides report that Contractor Foreman offers a 30-day free trial and a money-back guarantee on its larger plans, and that it does not offer month-to-month billing. Tradify offers a 14-day trial and then monthly per-user plans. For a small crew, per-user pricing can be cheaper; as the office and field team grows, a plan with several users included can work out better. Check current prices on both sites before deciding.

## Which is easier to learn?

Tradify, in our assessment. A learning curve and an overwhelming number of features are among the most common complaints about Contractor Foreman in public reviews, and reviewers say the mobile app does less than the web version.

Tradify reviewers more often praise its simplicity, with complaints centered on cost as the team grows and limited reporting.

## Can I use Contractor Foreman for service work?

You can, but it is not what it is built for. If most of your work is same-day service calls, the project tools add setup and clicks you will not use. Look at [Tradify](/reviews/tradify/), [Jobber](/reviews/jobber/) or [ServiceM8](/reviews/servicem8/) instead.
