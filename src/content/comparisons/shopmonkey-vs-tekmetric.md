## Which is better, Shopmonkey or Tekmetric?

They are close. In our assessment, Tekmetric has the clearer pricing because every plan includes unlimited users and repair orders, while Shopmonkey is the one reviewers more often call easy to learn. Book a demo of both with your own repair orders in mind.

## How does pricing compare?

Both publish tiered monthly pricing on their websites and discount annual billing, and both land in a similar range for a single shop. The structure differs more than the headline number.

Tekmetric's pricing page advertises unlimited users and unlimited repair orders on every plan, with month-to-month or discounted annual billing and no long-term contract. Third-party guides for Shopmonkey report user limits on the lower tier and digital inspections reserved for mid and upper tiers, and published Shopmonkey figures differ between listing sites. Ask each vendor for a written quote for your number of bays, technicians and locations, and confirm what is included. Prices change, so treat any figure you read as a starting point.

## Which one has better digital inspections?

Both offer digital vehicle inspections with photos sent to the customer. The practical difference is which plan includes them: Tekmetric lists inspections on its entry plan, while Shopmonkey pricing guides place them on higher tiers.

## Which integrates with more tools?

Tekmetric emphasizes its integration list, including parts ordering through platforms such as PartsTech and Nexpart, and labor data. Shopmonkey also integrates with parts suppliers, labor guides and QuickBooks. If you depend on a specific parts supplier, labor guide or accounting setup, ask each vendor to confirm the integration in writing.

## What do users complain about?

Common complaints in public reviews: for Shopmonkey, price, bugs reported after its 2.0 update, slow loading and a mobile app missing some web features; for Tekmetric, an interface some find busy, too many clicks for simple tasks and a learning curve that surprised shops at that price. Both also get plenty of praise, especially for support and inspections.

## What should I ask in the demos?

Ask how long a typical migration takes and what it costs, whether your parts suppliers and labor guide integrate, whether two-way texting is on your plan, and what happens to your data if you cancel. Full reviews: [Shopmonkey](/reviews/shopmonkey/) and [Tekmetric](/reviews/tekmetric/).
