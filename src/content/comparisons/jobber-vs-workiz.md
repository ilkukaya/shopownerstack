## Which is better, Jobber or Workiz?

Jobber is the better choice if your business wins work through quotes, and Workiz is the better choice if your business wins work through phone calls. Most small crews that quote and schedule recurring or planned work will find Jobber easier to adopt.

Both cover scheduling, estimates, invoicing, payments and a mobile app, so either will replace paper and spreadsheets. The deciding question is what happens before the job exists: a quote that needs a yes, or a phone call that needs an answer.

## How does pricing compare?

Jobber publishes its pricing and Workiz does not. As of mid-2026, Workiz lists its plans with a request-pricing button instead of prices.

Jobber sells tiered plans with a set number of users included and charges for extra seats. Its cheapest plan is aimed at a single user. Third-party pricing guides report that Workiz sells its phone system and Genius AI answering as add-ons on top of the base plan, which means the price you are quoted depends heavily on whether you want the phones. Ask for a written total that includes everything you plan to use, and confirm any contract term. Prices change, so check both vendor sites.

## Which one handles phone calls better?

Workiz. Its phone system records and summarizes calls and attaches them to the customer and job, according to Workiz's feature pages, and Genius Answering can answer missed calls and book work.

Jobber does not include a full business phone system. It does sell an AI Receptionist add-on that answers calls and texts and can book jobs, per its help center. That covers after-hours and overflow calls, but it is not the same as running your main business line through the software.

## Which one is better for quoting?

Jobber. Its help center documents optional line items that customers can select in the Client Hub, plus automatic follow-ups when a quote goes unanswered.

For businesses where a large share of quotes never get a response, that follow-up automation is often worth more than any other feature.

## What do users complain about?

For Jobber, common complaints in public reviews are about reporting depth and useful features being reserved for higher tiers. For Workiz, reviews on Capterra, Trustpilot and BBB include complaints about annual contract terms, billing after cancellation requests and slow support replies. Read the cancellation terms before you sign with any vendor.
