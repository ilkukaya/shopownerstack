HomeGuide reports that electricians charge $50 to $130 per hour on average, often with a service
call fee of $100 to $200 that covers the first hour. Master electricians doing specialty work
typically charge more. Rates vary with experience level and location.

Many residential electrical jobs repeat: panel upgrades, circuits, fixtures, EV chargers. In our
assessment, that makes a strong price book and saved material kits more valuable than an elaborate
dispatch board for a small electrical shop, because quotes go out faster and more consistently. If
permits and inspections are a regular part of your work, check how each tool lets you track them on
the job, since most field service software handles this with notes or custom fields.

The overhead figure in our calculator is an illustrative starting assumption, not an industry
statistic. Replace it with your own vehicle, insurance, licensing, software and other costs divided
by the hours you bill.
