Electrical work is more repeatable than most trades, which changes what good software looks
like. A shop that does the same twenty job types over and over gets more value from a strong
price book and saved kits than from a clever dispatch board. Compliance certificates are the
recurring gap: almost nothing in this category handles them properly.
