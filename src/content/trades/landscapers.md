Landscaping runs on route density: how many properties a crew can service between the yard
and home. Weather is the constant disruption, and the practical test of any product is how
long it takes to move a rained-off Tuesday into the rest of the week. Quoted design and
install work sits alongside the maintenance rounds and needs a different kind of paperwork.
