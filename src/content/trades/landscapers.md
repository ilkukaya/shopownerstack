HomeGuide reports that landscapers charge about $50 to $100 per hour for a two-person crew, or
roughly $25 to $50 per person. Many maintenance jobs are priced per visit instead, and larger
installs are usually quoted by project.

In our assessment, landscaping profit depends heavily on route density: how many properties a crew
can service between leaving the yard and coming back. Weather is the constant disruption, so a
useful test of any software is how easily you can move a rained-out day's visits into the rest of the
week. Quoted design and install work sits alongside recurring maintenance and needs proper quotes,
deposits and job costing.

The overhead figure in our calculator is an illustrative starting assumption, not an industry
statistic. Replace it with your own equipment, fuel, insurance, software and other costs divided by
the hours you bill.
