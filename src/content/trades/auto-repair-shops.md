AAA reports that most US repair shops charge between $120 and $159 an hour for labor, with
rates ranging from about $47 to $215 depending on location and the vehicles a shop services.
Labor rates are set by local costs and competition, so check what shops near you charge before you
use the figure above.

In our assessment, an independent repair shop has two levers software can help with. The first is
authorization: how much recommended work the customer approves. Digital vehicle inspections with
photos and text-to-approve estimates are the main tools here. The second is parts margin, which is
easier to protect when markup follows a matrix set in the software rather than a guess at the
counter.

The overhead figure in our calculator is an illustrative starting assumption, not an industry
statistic. Replace it with your own rent, insurance, software and other fixed costs divided by the
hours you actually bill.
