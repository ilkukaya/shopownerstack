Thumbtack reports that personal trainers typically charge $40 to $100 per hour, with an average
around $55. Studios mostly sell memberships and class packs rather than hours, so treat this as a
rough benchmark for one-to-one sessions, not a studio pricing guide.

In our assessment, a studio is a subscription business run on a timetable. Class capacity and
instructor cost set the ceiling on revenue, and retention decides whether the studio grows. Failed
recurring card payments are one way members lapse without choosing to leave, so check how each
platform retries failed payments and notifies members before you compare features on the marketing
page. Also compare payment processing rates, which add up quickly on recurring billing.

The overhead figure in our calculator is an illustrative starting assumption, not an industry
statistic. Replace it with your own rent, equipment, insurance, software and other costs divided by
the hours you run sessions or classes.
