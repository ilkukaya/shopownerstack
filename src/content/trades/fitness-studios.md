A studio is a subscription business wearing a timetable. Class capacity and instructor cost
set the ceiling, but the number that decides whether the studio survives is churn - and a
large share of churn is not people deciding to leave, it is cards failing and nobody chasing
them. Judge fitness software on its dunning before you judge it on anything else.
