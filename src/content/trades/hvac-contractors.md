HomeGuide reports that HVAC repairs cost $75 to $150 per hour, with a minimum service call fee of
$75 to $200 that most companies apply to the first hour. Emergency work at night, on weekends or on
holidays is typically billed at higher rates.

HVAC work is strongly seasonal. In our assessment, the practical test for software is whether
scheduling and dispatch hold up when call volume spikes in the first heat wave or cold snap of the
year. Maintenance agreements are the other half of the business: recurring visits should schedule
themselves, or they tend to slip. For replacement jobs, check whether the software supports good,
better, best options on quotes and consumer financing.

The overhead figure in our calculator is an illustrative starting assumption, not an industry
statistic. Replace it with your own vehicle, tools, insurance, software and other costs divided by
the hours you bill.
