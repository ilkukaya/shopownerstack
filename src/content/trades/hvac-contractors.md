HVAC has the sharpest seasonality of any trade on this site. A system that copes with a
normal Tuesday can fall apart in the first week of a heatwave, when the phone rings all day
and every job is urgent. Maintenance agreements are the other half of the picture: recurring
visits that have to schedule themselves, or they simply do not happen.
