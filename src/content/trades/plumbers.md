HomeGuide reports that plumbers charge $45 to $150 per hour, with a minimum service call or trip
fee of $50 to $200. Emergency calls at night or on weekends are billed at higher rates. Many plumbers
also use flat-rate pricing for common jobs.

In our assessment, plumbing is a demanding test for field service software because the work splits
in two. Part of it is unplanned emergency calls that need dispatching quickly, and part is quoted
replacement work, such as water heaters or repipes, that needs a price in front of the customer
before they call someone else. Look for fast dispatch, a mobile price book and on-site quoting with
options and e-signature.

The overhead figure in our calculator is an illustrative starting assumption, not an industry
statistic. Replace it with your own vehicle, tools, insurance, software and other costs divided by
the hours you bill.
