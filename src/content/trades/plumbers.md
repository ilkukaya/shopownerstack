Plumbing is the hardest test of field service software because the work splits in two. Half
of it is unplanned emergency calls that need dispatching in minutes; the other half is
quoted replacement work that needs a price on paper before the customer changes their mind.
Software that only does one of those will get abandoned within a quarter.
