HireRush's cost guide puts hair stylists at about $40 to $160 per hour, with an average around $95.
Most salons price by service rather than by the hour, so use this as a rough benchmark for what a
chair can earn in an hour.

In our assessment, a salon's economics come down to chair hours sold against chair hours available.
That makes three things worth tracking: no-shows, gaps between appointments and how many clients
rebook before they leave. When comparing salon software, look at deposits or card-on-file booking,
automated reminders, and prompts to rebook at checkout. Software that only stores appointments will
not move any of those numbers.

The overhead figure in our calculator is an illustrative starting assumption, not an industry
statistic. Replace it with your own rent or chair rental, products, insurance, software and other
costs divided by the hours you book.
