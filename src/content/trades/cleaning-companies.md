Cleaning is a recurring-visit business with a workforce that changes constantly. Two things
matter more than anything else: the recurring schedule has to generate itself for months
ahead without anyone rebooking by hand, and a new cleaner has to be productive in the app on
their first shift. Margins are thin enough that per-seat pricing is a genuine constraint.
