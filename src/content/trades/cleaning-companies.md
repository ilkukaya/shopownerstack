HomeGuide puts house cleaning at $25 to $50 per hour per cleaner, with many companies quoting a
flat price per visit based on home size instead. Rates depend on the type of clean, the size of the
home and how often the customer books.

Cleaning is a recurring-visit business, and in our assessment two things matter most when choosing
software. Recurring schedules should generate automatically for months ahead, so nobody rebooks
regular customers by hand. And the mobile app should be simple enough that a new cleaner can use it
on their first shift, because cleaning teams often change. With margins this thin, per-user pricing is
worth checking carefully before you commit.

The overhead figure in our calculator is an illustrative starting assumption, not an industry
statistic. Replace it with your own supplies, insurance, vehicle, software and other costs divided by
the hours you bill.
