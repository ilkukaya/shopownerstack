## What is Momence?

Momence is booking and business software for class-based studios, including yoga, pilates, dance and
boutique fitness, plus some wellness and appointment businesses. It covers class and appointment
booking, memberships and packages, payments, marketing automation, livestreaming and an on-demand
video library.

Unlike [Mindbody](/reviews/mindbody/), it focuses on your own branded booking experience rather than
a shared consumer marketplace.

## How much does Momence cost?

Momence does not publish subscription pricing. Its pricing page lists payment processing rates by
country and routes subscription pricing through a demo request, as of our check on 2026-09-26.

Several third-party sites quote Momence plan prices, but the figures conflict with each other, so we
have not listed any. What is published is US online card processing at 3.9% plus $0.30. On a studio
taking $20,000 a month in online payments, for example, every percentage point of processing is $200
a month, so include processing in any comparison. Ask for a written quote and confirm on Momence's
site.

## What features stand out?

Marketing automation and online content are the main differentiators. According to Momence, the
platform includes behavior-based automations, email and SMS campaigns and lead capture, so a studio
can run win-back and follow-up messages without a separate marketing tool. Livestreaming and an
on-demand video library let studios sell online classes alongside in-person memberships.

## What do users like about Momence?

Positive reviews tend to mention the breadth of features for the price of one platform, the modern
client booking flow, and having marketing tools in the same system as the schedule.

## What are the common complaints?

The common complaints in public reviews are support quality, billing and pricing changes. Momence
holds about 3.9 out of 5 on Capterra from roughly 75 reviews at the time of writing, with customer
service among its lower sub-scores. Reviewers mention slow or inconsistent support after onboarding,
billing discrepancies, unexpected pricing changes, buggy features and reporting inaccuracies. Its G2
review base is too small to draw conclusions from.

## What should you ask in a Momence demo?

Since pricing is not published, the demo is where you find out what you will pay. Questions worth
asking:

- What is the monthly subscription for your member count and locations, and is it locked for the term?
- Are there per-transaction platform fees on top of card processing, and who pays them, you or the
  client?
- Is livestreaming and on-demand video included, or an add-on?
- What is the contract length and the cancellation process?
- What support is available after onboarding, and through which channels?
- How are active memberships and billing dates migrated from your current system?

Get the quote in writing. Several complaints in public reviews involve pricing or billing changes
after signing.

## Momence or Mindbody?

In our assessment, Momence suits a studio that wants to own its booking brand and run its own
marketing, and Mindbody suits one that values marketplace discovery. Both route most pricing through
sales, so compare written quotes including processing fees. For salons and med spas focused on
one-to-one appointments, look at [Boulevard](/reviews/boulevard/) instead.

This is an editorial rating based on Momence's published information and on themes in public reviews
on Capterra and G2, using the rubric on our [how we test](/how-we-test/) page. We did not run timed
tests. Momence scores well on scheduling and communication, and lower on value and reporting because
pricing is not published, processing is relatively expensive and reviewers report reporting issues.
