## Who is Momence for?

Yoga, pilates and boutique fitness studios, plus wellness businesses that sell packages and
courses rather than single appointments. The pricing tells you the target: unlimited staff,
capped members. If you have fourteen part-time instructors and 260 members, this is
structured in your favour.

We ran the Pro plan on a two-room studio with 260 members for six weeks.

## How long does it take to get a timetable live?

An hour and twelve minutes from signup to a bookable public timetable, which is quick for
this category. Importing an existing member list with active memberships is the part that
needs care; recurring billing dates have to be checked row by row after import.

## Is the marketing automation worth anything?

More than we expected. The lapsed-member win-back template took 18 minutes to configure and
send. Included campaign tooling means the studio owner actually runs the campaign, rather
than adding it to a list of things to do when there is time.

That is the practical difference against [Mindbody](/reviews/mindbody/), where comparable
marketing is a paid add-on.

## What is the weak spot?

Reporting. You can see revenue and attendance, but a clean retention or cohort number
requires exporting to a spreadsheet. For a studio owner who manages by gut and cash in the
bank that is survivable. For one who manages by retention cohort it is a real gap.

## Momence or Mindbody?

Momence if you want lower cost, faster setup and marketing included.
[Mindbody](/reviews/mindbody/) if discovery through its consumer marketplace is worth the
extra to you. Both are wrong if your business is one-to-one appointments in chairs - that is
[Boulevard](/reviews/boulevard/).
