## What is Mindbody?

Mindbody is scheduling, booking and payments software for fitness studios, gyms, yoga studios, spas
and wellness businesses. Its main difference from competitors is the Mindbody consumer app, where
people find and book classes and appointments.

Mindbody was founded in San Luis Obispo, California, and became an LLC in 2001. It acquired ClassPass
in 2021, and both now sit under a parent brand called Playlist.

## How much does Mindbody cost?

Mindbody says its US pricing starts at $79 a month per location for the Starter plan. Accelerate,
Ultimate and Ultimate Plus are quote-based, based on the vendor's pricing pages checked on 2026-09-26.

Pricing is per location, so a multi-location business multiplies the cost. Third-party pricing
guides also note payment processing fees, possible fees on marketplace bookings and paid add-ons
such as a branded mobile app. We have not listed those figures as plan prices because they are not
published by Mindbody on its pricing page. Ask for the total monthly cost in writing, including
processing and add-ons.

## What does each tier add?

According to Mindbody, Starter covers scheduling, online booking, payments, client records, staff
coordination and marketplace discovery. Accelerate adds more advanced scheduling, reporting and
operational controls. Ultimate adds marketing automation, lead management and retention tools.
Ultimate Plus is the top tier.

## What do users like about Mindbody?

Users most often value the marketplace exposure and the breadth of the platform. Mindbody has
thousands of reviews on Capterra with an overall rating of about 4.0 out of 5 at the time of writing.
Positive themes include reliable class and appointment scheduling, clients who already have the app,
and detailed reporting once it is set up.

## What are the common complaints?

The common complaints in public reviews are cost increases, support and contract terms. On Capterra,
value for money and customer service are among Mindbody's lowest sub-scores. Reviewers describe
prices rising over time, add-ons that increase the bill, support that feels scripted, and difficulty
leaving because client data, payments and schedules are all tied to the platform.

In our assessment these complaints are consistent enough that any studio should read the contract
closely before signing. Our [Mindbody alternatives](/alternatives/mindbody/) page covers options.

## What should you ask before signing a Mindbody contract?

Because most tiers are quote-based, the contract is where the real cost is set. Questions worth asking
before you sign:

- What is the total monthly cost for your locations, including processing and any add-ons?
- How long is the initial term, does it auto-renew, and what does early cancellation cost?
- What fees apply to clients who book through the marketplace?
- Is there a price increase cap at renewal?
- Can you export client, membership and payment data if you leave, and is there a fee for it?

Get every answer in writing. Several of the complaints in public reviews are about costs or terms the
reviewer says they only discovered later.

## Mindbody, Momence or Boulevard?

For a class-based studio, [Momence](/reviews/momence/) is the main modern alternative. It focuses on
your own branded booking and marketing rather than a shared marketplace. For salons, spas and med spas
built around one-to-one appointments, [Boulevard](/reviews/boulevard/) is the stronger fit; see
[Boulevard vs Mindbody](/compare/boulevard-vs-mindbody/).

This is an editorial rating based on Mindbody's published pricing and plan descriptions and on themes
in public reviews on Capterra and G2, using the rubric on our [how we test](/how-we-test/) page. We
did not run timed tests. Mindbody scores well on scheduling and reporting and lowest on value, because
most tier prices are not public and reviewers frequently report cost creep.
