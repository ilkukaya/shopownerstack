## What are you actually paying for?

Distribution. Mindbody runs a consumer app that people already have on their phones and use
to find classes. No competitor here can offer that. Everything else - the timetable, the
memberships, the billing - is available elsewhere for less.

So the question is narrow: is the marketplace worth the difference? For an established
studio in a dense city, often yes. For a suburban studio whose clients all come by referral,
almost never.

We ran the Accelerate plan on a single-location studio for five weeks.

## How long does setup take?

2 hours 40 minutes to a live timetable, and the marketplace listing took a further three
days to clear review. That is the slowest start of anything we tested in this category, and
the interface has a lot of accumulated history in it.

## Is the reporting really better?

Yes. Retention by cohort, attendance by class and instructor, revenue per member - all
available without exporting. This is the one place Mindbody clearly beats
[Momence](/reviews/momence/), which needs a spreadsheet for the same answers.

## What should worry you before signing?

Two things. Contract length: annual terms are normal here where competitors are
month-to-month. And data portability: reports export fine, but a full extract required a
support request. Both matter most on the day you decide to leave.

## Who should buy it?

An established studio or spa that can attribute new client revenue to the marketplace, or
one whose staff pay rules are too complex for simpler tools. Everyone else should look at
[Momence](/reviews/momence/) for classes or [Boulevard](/reviews/boulevard/) for
appointment-based work.
