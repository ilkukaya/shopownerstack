## Is Tradify worth it for a small trade business?

For a small electrical, plumbing or building team that wants quoting, job tracking and timesheets without a steep learning curve, yes. Users on Capterra rate it around 4.6-4.7 out of 5 and repeatedly mention ease of use and quoting speed.

Its limits are cost as the team grows, because pricing is per user, and thinner customer-facing tools than [Jobber](/reviews/jobber/) or [Housecall Pro](/reviews/housecall-pro/).

## How much does Tradify cost in 2026?

Capterra's 2026 pricing listing shows US prices of $47 per user per month for Lite, $51 for Pro and $61 for Plus, with a Custom plan on request. Tradify's help centre says pricing depends on region, number of users and add-ons, and that annual billing options are available.

Per-user pricing is simple to understand but scales directly with headcount. A hypothetical five-person team on Plus would pay about $305 a month at those rates, which is more than several tier-based plans that include five users. Prices change, so check Tradify's site.

## What does Tradify do well?

Quoting and time tracking. Reviewers frequently say quotes that used to take a long time now take minutes, and that crews like logging time on their phones instead of handing in paper timesheets at the end of the week.

The app covers the core job cycle for a trade business: enquiry, quote, job, schedule, timesheet and invoice, with job costing on the higher plans. Tradify was founded in New Zealand by an electrical engineer, and its focus on electricians, plumbers and builders shows in the feature set.

Because the field app runs on both iOS and Android, a mixed crew can use it without anyone buying a new phone. That is a practical advantage over [ServiceM8](/reviews/servicem8/), whose full app is iPhone-first. Accounting sync with Xero and QuickBooks Online means invoices raised on site do not need to be re-keyed by whoever does the books.

The 14-day trial includes all features, so the sensible way to evaluate it is to put one real week of quotes, jobs and timesheets through it, then check how long invoicing took and whether the crew actually logged their hours.

## What are the common complaints about Tradify?

Public reviews on Capterra and G2 raise a consistent set of issues:

- **Features held back for Plus.** Purchase orders, bulk invoicing, kit bundles, staff activity history, job service reports and reporting sit on the top plan.
- **Limited template customisation.** Quotes and invoices can be branded, but layouts are limited.
- **Reporting.** Some users say built-in reports are not what they need day to day, and a few mention slowdowns on large jobs.

## How does Tradify compare with ServiceM8 and Contractor Foreman?

[ServiceM8](/reviews/servicem8/) charges by job volume, has a free plan and is strongest on iPhone. Tradify charges per user and supports iOS and Android equally. [Contractor Foreman](/reviews/contractor-foreman/) is built for longer projects with estimating, change orders and job costing. See [ServiceM8 vs Tradify](/compare/servicem8-vs-tradify/) and [Tradify vs Contractor Foreman](/compare/tradify-vs-contractor-foreman/).

A trade business with one to about eight people doing a mix of service calls and small projects, where the owner wants quoting and timesheets sorted.
